My Design Choices

For this project, I chose a dark navy and purple color scheme to create a modern and visually appealing design. I organized the website into separate sections to make it easy to navigate and distinguish my academic interests from my personal interests. The two images represent my interest in anime and my experience as a student at Binghamton University. I chose a bar chart to display my anime ratings because it makes comparing the different shows easy. I also included a line graph to demonstrate how data can be used to show changes over time. Overall, I wanted the portfolio to reflect my personality while maintaining a clean and organized appearance.
